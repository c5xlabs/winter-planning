# Evidence

Part of `initiative:lab`; its requirements are not yet written.

## The problem

The fleet already proposes changes to itself: a retrospective names a recurring failure, a garden pass drafts a
proposal. Today such a proposal is judged on argument alone, and once a change is adopted, whether it keeps helping is
noticed only by feel.

## The outcome

A proposal to change a prompt, a graph, or a convention can carry an experiment run on the lab, so the person approving
it reads evidence beside the case. Once a change is adopted, the lab keeps watching it: a small share of live chunks
gets a shadow trial on the previous configuration, never landing, so a change that helped in the lab and quietly hurts
in the field is noticed by the fleet before its owner notices it by feel.
