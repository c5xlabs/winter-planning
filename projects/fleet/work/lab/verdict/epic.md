# Verdict

Part of `initiative:lab`; its requirements are in `requirements.md`.

## The problem

Trials are only as useful as their reading. `persona:harness-engineer` needs to know whether one arm did better than
another, by how much, and at what cost, from a reading no agent wrote and no lucky streak decided.

## The outcome

Some of what the verdict reads the fleet already records as facts and costs nothing to collect. A plan-review that waves
a weak plan through and a plan-review that loops it four times leave very different traces, and the count of loops per
gate may be the single most honest number the lab produces.

Other readings need judgement, and the lab supplies it by graph, so a judge is a node like any other and can itself be
put on a bench later. The verdict reports effect sizes with their uncertainty, not a winner, and it is willing to say
*inconclusive*.
