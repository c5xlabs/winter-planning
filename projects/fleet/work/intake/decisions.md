# Backlog intake decisions

## Search across sources (decision:intake/search-scope)

**Open.** What search means across a hub of several projects and sources with different query languages: one federated
box, or one source at a time; it blocks `product:backlog-browsing`.

## What ungrouping does (decision:intake/ungroup-shape)

**Open.** Whether ungrouping is a real split of a chunk or a re-ingest of its parts, and what happens to facts already
recorded against the folded chunk; it blocks `product:ungroup-chunk`.

## Browsable work sources (decision:intake/source-enumeration)

Browsing is a loosening of the work-source seam: the seam gains an optional capability to enumerate and search, a
deliberate departure from id-addressed ingest, decided in this epic.
