# UI redesign

A standalone epic with no product requirements yet: eight design proposals explore the space before any requirement is
decided.

## The problem

The operator, `persona:application-architect` first and `persona:harness-engineer` beside them, queues work on the
fleet, walks away, and comes back to see what landed, what is stuck, and what it cost. The board's first design proved
what they need on one screen: what is running, what is stuck, what is waiting on them, and what it all cost. It is dense
and honest about cost, and it shows live movement. Its hierarchy is flat, though. The two chunks that need a human weigh
the same as the fifty-three that are ready, so the ones that need the operator are buried. Colour decorates rather than
signals, everything is set in one monospace face at one weight in capitals, and the whole reads as a generic dark
dashboard rather than an instrument shaped to the work.

## The outcome

The hub board and the fleet view get a second design, built for the operator as the power user they are, on the desk and
on the phone. It replaces the first design's visual language and hierarchy and keeps its density and its truth. At the
desk it is a keyboard-first instrument the operator reads at a glance and acts from without hunting, and when something
goes wrong they can take it over in one step. On the phone it answers one question, whether the fleet needs them, and
lets them act on the answer.

Eight proposals answer a shared brief, each with a concept, working desktop and mobile mocks of the board and the fleet,
and rendered screenshots. Three take the modern frontier, two deliberately work outside current convention, and three
are classic SaaS: a control to measure the others against, and two variations on it drawn from other families of SaaS
product. The brief, each concept, its mocks, and a comparison of the eight sit in
[`research/redesign-proposals/`](./research/redesign-proposals/comparison.md), and the first design as captured from the
live hub in [`assets/baseline/`](./assets/baseline/).
