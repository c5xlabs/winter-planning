# Retention decisions

## What expiry does to a record (decision:retention/expiry-form)

**Open.** Whether expiry is deletion or relegation to something colder and cheaper the hub no longer has to open; it
blocks `product:retention-policy`.

## Expiry as a hub sweep (decision:retention/expiry-as-sweep)

Letting go of old records is the hub's own housekeeping over its own rows, so it runs as a hub sweep rather than a
cadence, because a cadence mints work for runners.
